Brooklyn Nets Visioning

Overview

Goal

Following the success of the New York Liberty app, we were tasked with reimagining the Brooklyn Nets fan experience using that same product foundation — but making it feel unique to the Nets, not just a re-skin.

Solution

We explored the Nets’ brand and looked for opportunities to root the experience in it, while maintaining the feature set the dev team had already built out from the Liberty work.
